Retry evidence lab · fixed synthetic examples

The last call is not
the whole task.

An agent can eventually complete a task—and still leave an earlier violation, extra costs, and a long wait behind it.

Keep the whole declared retry history. Review task outcomes and operating limits independently. Download the evidence and recompute every result offline.

All scores, costs, timings, and histories here are constructed. They are not measured performance of a model, company, agency, or production service. “Repaired” describes a fixture, not a proven real-world repair.

01 · Follow one logical task

Eventual completion does not erase history.

This task declares 3 sequential calls. The final call completes, but an earlier call records an unauthorized action. The plan takes the final completion score and the maximum unauthorized-action score across all calls.

The other logical task has one call. Attempt indices and complete declared counts are checked. This does not prove external events were all captured, nor that repeating an action is safe.

04 · Verify, then adapt

No keys. Complete offline evidence.

Download any 31-file handoff and extract its complete folder structure. Use the matching current repository harness or a wheel built from it; these commands are not in published PyPI 1.6.0.

python -m pip install -e harness

# Verify a downloaded handoff, including its budget layer
aau retry-review verify /path/to/extracted-handoff --require-budget

# Or generate all three examples and an adoption guide offline
aau retry-demo --out /path/to/new-retry-demo

The earlier-harm and slow-completion handoffs exit 1: their complete evidence verifies, but constraints fail. The repaired fixture exits 0. Exit 2 means invalid, incomplete, modified, or incompatible evidence. The browser page does not verify itself.

Each handoff retains both ledgers, accounting plans, recordings, evaluations, policy, diagnostics, candidate budget, separate verdicts, combined gate, HTML, and manifests. Verification recomputes the entire chain, not just hashes. An unrelated but individually valid nested review cannot replace the linked evidence.

Bring independently reviewed definitions to CI

Approve both accounting plans, the baseline ledger, policy, diagnostics, and any required budget outside candidate-controlled changes. The Action checks exact-byte pins and matching declared context. Different metric reducers are refused even when context hashes match. Identical reducers can still be unsuitable; a last-only reducer may hide earlier harm on both sides.

Limits and research context

AWS on timeouts, retries, and backoff explains why retries add work and why a timeout need not mean no side effect occurred. The accounting contract here is this repository’s design, not an AWS standard or endorsement.

Only declared sequential, non-overlapping attempt accounting is supported. Do not flatten nested spans, parallel work, hedged requests, mixed currencies, or mixed cost bases into it. Source JSON values are retained, not original file whitespace. Hashes do not prove authenticity, complete collection, scoring validity, accurate invoices, or deployment approval. Retain the matching harness revision.

No retry is executed or authorized by this lesson. Only fixed synthetic fixtures are published. General-purpose handoffs retain unsanitized source values; inspect your own files before sharing.

No scripts, analytics, external fonts, model calls, or source uploads. Generated from the tested accounting reports.
