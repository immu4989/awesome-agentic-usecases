# Federal AI Portfolio Observatory — research and boundary notes

Last verified: **2026-08-23**
Next source review: **2026-11-23**

## Problem selection

The Observatory implements four evidence-backed gaps:

1. Federal AI inventory entries can be incomplete, omit expected benefit, and fail to map
   investments to strategic outcomes or performance metrics.
2. Similar capabilities can be initiated across business units without portfolio-level
   visibility.
3. Acquisition obligations for data rights, privacy, testing, pricing, portability,
   accessibility, incidents, and exit are useful only when teams can test them.
4. Model testing alone is not contextual evaluation; red teaming and realistic human field
   simulation provide distinct evidence.

## Deliberate boundaries

- Similarity is a deterministic review signal, never proof of duplication.
- The system never recommends funding, cancellation, consolidation, award, acceptance,
  deployment, retirement, fraud findings, eligibility, or other protected action.
- Public-value changes are recomputed only from declared baseline and observed values.
  They are not causal estimates or audited savings.
- Field simulation uses synthetic tasks and aggregate reporting. It is not production
  validation.
- Clause records are test scaffolds, not contract language, legal advice, or a compliance
  conclusion.
- Public tools accept only public or synthetic records after affirmative human publication
  review and negative sensitive-data attestations.
