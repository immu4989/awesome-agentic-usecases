# 🏠 Insurance & Disaster Recovery

Disaster survivors often move through private insurance, public assistance, and charitable
help at the same time. Agents in this industry must reduce repeated paperwork without
hiding compensation, inventing eligibility, or allowing a filing window to expire.

| Verified lab | Question | Specialty |
|---|---|---|
| [Disaster Claim and Aid Coordinator](disaster-claim-aid-coordinator/) | Can an agent coordinate the next safe recovery step while declaring every known compensation source? | `public-value` `source-coordination` `deadline` `human-in-loop` |

All policies, declarations, claims, payments, households, and records in the lab are
fictional. It is not insurance, FEMA, SBA, tax, benefits, or legal guidance.
