# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be kind. Argue with the measurement, not the person who made it.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where people
interact about this project.

## Expected

- **Disagree with evidence.** This is a repository about measurement. "I think that number
  is wrong, here is why" is the most valuable contribution anyone can make, and it is
  always welcome regardless of who wrote the number.
- **Assume good faith.** Most disagreements here are about methodology, and most
  methodological mistakes are honest.
- **Be specific.** Vague criticism is hard to act on; a reproducing command is easy.

## Not acceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments about a person rather than their work.
- Discrimination or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Deliberately wasting maintainer time in bad faith.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer via a GitHub issue, or privately through
[GitHub's report abuse flow](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) if the issue involves
the maintainer or is sensitive. Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences escalate
from a warning to a temporary block to a permanent ban, depending on severity and pattern.

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
