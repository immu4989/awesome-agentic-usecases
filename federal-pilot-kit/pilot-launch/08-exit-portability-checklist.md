# 08 — Exit, portability, and continuity rehearsal

Rehearse exit before the Day-30 decision. A written promise is not a portability result.

- [ ] Inventory agency data, prompts, outputs, tests, evaluation results, configurations, logs,
  documentation, model/provider identifiers, access grants, and integrations.
- [ ] Export each required artifact in the declared format and verify completeness, schema,
  timestamps, hashes, readability, and reuse rights.
- [ ] Reproduce one critical workflow from the export without hidden vendor state.
- [ ] Restore the prior or replacement process inside the recovery-time and data-loss bounds.
- [ ] Disable service accounts, tokens, webhooks, network routes, users, subprocessors, and support
  access; reconcile the final access inventory.
- [ ] Apply approved retention, legal hold, records, deletion, backup, and incident obligations;
  retain the proper evidence without copying protected data into the public repository.
- [ ] Reconcile final usage, transition assistance, overage, export, storage, deletion, and early-
  termination charges to the declared pricing units.
- [ ] Identify unresolved defects, incidents, appeals/remedies, records requests, and affected-user
  communications that survive termination.
- [ ] Obtain human sign-off from program, data, security/privacy, records, acquisition, operations,
  and continuity owners as applicable.

Record elapsed time, manual effort, failed steps, data loss, unavailable formats, vendor help,
unexpected cost, residual access, continuity impact, and the owner/date for every unresolved item.
The pilot cannot pass its portability gate if the rehearsal did not occur.
