# 06 — Acquisition and commercial review

Use these as questions for authorized acquisition and counsel review, not as model clauses.

- What measurable mission outcome and intended environment does each performance term bind to?
- Which evidence is due before award, before pilot use, after a change, after an incident, and at
  exit? Who may reproduce it?
- What government data, prompts, outputs, feedback, logs, derived data, embeddings, configurations,
  and evaluation artifacts may each party use, retain, disclose, or train on?
- Which models, services, subprocessors, locations, licenses, and material dependencies may change?
  What notice, retest, objection, and cease-use rights follow?
- Which unit prices, volume tiers, minimums, overages, implementation work, human review, monitoring,
  incident work, transition assistance, and exit charges determine lifecycle cost?
- Can the agency export usable data, configuration, prompts, tests, logs, and documentation in an
  open or specified format within a measured time? What requires vendor cooperation?
- What accessibility, security, privacy, records, audit, incident, remedy, and continuity evidence
  is inspectable without exposing protected information?
- What happens to service, data, access, intellectual property, and unresolved incidents at
  suspension, expiration, termination, vendor failure, or provider/model withdrawal?

Record every assumption and exception beside its owner and verification method. A complete response
is not a favorable rating, and this kit never compares offerors or recommends an award.
