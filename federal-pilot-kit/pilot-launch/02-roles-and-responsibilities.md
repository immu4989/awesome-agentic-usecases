# 02 — Roles and decision rights

Assign one accountable owner per decision. A vendor may provide evidence but cannot own agency
acceptance, risk, privacy, security, records, or award decisions.

| Decision or work product | Responsible | Accountable | Consulted | Informed | Cannot perform |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mission outcome and baseline | Program team | Program official | Affected users, data owner | Pilot team | Responder alone |
| High-impact and rights review | Designated agency reviewers | Authorized official | Counsel, civil-rights/accessibility staff | Program official | Pilot system |
| Data approval and minimization | Data steward | Data owner | Privacy, security, records | Test team | Responder by default |
| Test manifest and hidden cases | Independent evaluator | Test owner | Domain experts | Program official | Responding system team |
| Security and privacy acceptance | Control owners | Authorized agency officials | System owner, vendor | Pilot team | Repository tooling |
| Requirement evidence response | Responder | Responder lead | Program and test teams | Reviewer | Reviewer writing evidence for responder |
| Incident triage and cease use | Operations lead | Named stop authority | Security, privacy, program | Stakeholders | Agent autonomously |
| Day-30 decision | Review team prepares | Accountable official decides | All control owners | Stakeholders | CLI, browser desk, vendor |

Name a backup for each accountable role, an escalation route, the intervention point before any
protected action, and the person authorized to stop the pilot immediately. Preserve separation
between requirements, response evidence, independent tests, and the final decision.
