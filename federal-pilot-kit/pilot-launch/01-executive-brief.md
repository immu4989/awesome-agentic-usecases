## Decision statement

On Day 30, the accountable official will choose **continue**, **revise**, **stop**, or **bounded
extension** based on the predeclared measures and complete gap ledger. The pilot will not make an
award, benefits, enforcement, eligibility, safety, clinical, employment, payment, rights, or risk-
acceptance decision.

## Approval record

Keep names and dates only inside the agency's approved system. In a public fork, use role labels
and synthetic dates. Approval to run a bounded pilot is not approval to deploy.
