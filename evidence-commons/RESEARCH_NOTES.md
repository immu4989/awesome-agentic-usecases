## Evidence spine

The contract maps current federal evidence needs into portable public artifacts:

| Source signal | Contract response | Deliberate limit |
|---|---|---|
| OMB M-25-21 asks for expected benefit compared with existing agency processes, lifecycle reassessment, costs, and independent review | Same-suite agent and aggregate human evidence, predeclared value measures, transfer triggers, and reproduction state remain separate | The capsule does not establish M-25-21 compliance or authorize deployment |
| GAO organizes AI accountability around governance, data, performance, and monitoring | Human authority, source-bound artifacts, outcome measures, limitations, and reassessment triggers are required | No composite trust or readiness score is calculated |
| GAO's evidence guide emphasizes planning, building, using, and learning from evidence | Open gaps and the next required artifact are first-class output | Artifact presence does not prove methodological quality |
| NIST ARIA separates model testing, red teaming, and field testing | Synthetic agent evidence cannot silently become a human or real-world claim | AAU does not call a small comparator an ARIA field test |
| HHS OHRP explains that purpose and design govern whether human protections may apply | Observed human evidence needs a contributor-attested institutional basis; sessions are never published | AAU cannot verify or substitute for the institution's determination |

## Premise corrections made during implementation

1. **“A model receipt next to a suite is suite-bound.” — Incorrect.** The three historical
   receipts contain scenario identifiers but no suite hash, and the current committed suites have
   expanded from 8 to 32 cases. The reference capsules label the binding `scenario_ids_only` and
   require a fresh hash-bound rerun.
2. **“Aggregate human data proves impact.” — Incorrect.** It supports a same-task descriptive
   comparator. Public-value outcomes, operational burden, harms, costs, and workflow effects need
   their own declared observation.
3. **“Before/after proves causality.” — Incorrect.** The public-value record distinguishes
   descriptive methods from a declared causal evaluation and preserves uncertainty and limits.
4. **“Independent means verified.” — Incorrect.** A reproduction record can attest organizational
   separation; this repository cannot verify the person or organization behind it.
5. **“More evidence means permission to automate.” — Incorrect.** Protected authority remains with
   accountable people regardless of capsule status.

## Interpretation boundary

An Impact Capsule is a public evidence index and portable integrity pack. It is not a program
evaluation standard, audit opinion, scientific peer review, institutional-review determination,
privacy assessment, accessibility conformance report, legal opinion, procurement decision,
certification, vendor ranking, worker assessment, or government endorsement.
